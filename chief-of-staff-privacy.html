Privacy Policy

This policy explains how FounderFlow collects, uses, and protects personal information submitted through the website and onboarding flow.

1) Data We Collect

  • Contact details you submit in forms (name, email, WhatsApp number, role, company context).
  • Operational details you share for service onboarding and delivery.
  • Marketing attribution details such as UTM parameters and referrer.

2) How We Use Data

  • To contact you about onboarding, delivery, billing, and support.
  • To provide and improve AI Chief of Staff service outcomes.
  • To measure campaign performance and lead quality.

3) Payments

Card payments are processed by Paystack. FounderFlow does not store full card details. Payment handling is subject to Paystack security and compliance controls.

4) Data Sharing

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We may share limited information with service providers only where necessary for delivery (for example payment processing or communication tools).

5) Data Retention

We keep client and lead records only for as long as needed for service, billing, legal, and support purposes.
