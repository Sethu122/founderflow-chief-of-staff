Refund & Cancellation Policy

This policy applies to all FounderFlow AI Chief of Staff services purchased via Paystack.

1) Validation Sprint (Pilot) - One-Time Service

  • Full refund if cancellation is requested before kickoff and within 48 hours of payment.
  • If kickoff has started and delivery work has begun, the Pilot fee is non-refundable.
  • If FounderFlow cannot start service within 5 business days after confirmed onboarding, client may request a full refund.

2) Operator Partner - Monthly Engagement

  • Billing is monthly in advance for the service period.
  • Client may cancel renewal any time before the next billing date to prevent future charges.
  • For the active month already in progress, partial refunds are generally not provided unless service delivery failed materially.

3) Service Dissatisfaction and Disputes

  • Clients should first contact support to request resolution within 7 days of the issue.
  • FounderFlow investigates and responds within 2 business days with either remediation steps or refund decision.
  • If a chargeback/dispute is raised via Paystack, FounderFlow will provide delivery records and communication history as proof of service.

4) How to Request Cancellation or Refund

Send request details from the purchase email address to muchangasethu0@gmail.com and include payment receipt, plan name, and reason for cancellation/refund request.

5) Processing Timelines

  • Approved refunds are initiated within 3 business days.
  • Final settlement timing depends on the customer bank/card network.
